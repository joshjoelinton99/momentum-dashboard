Rules

Each line is the rule this run actually used. Edit PullbackParams in momentum_pullback.py, or pass the flags in the command at the bottom.

  1. Universe. Current S&P 500 + Nasdaq 100 names. Index membership is the historical stand-in for market cap above $100M; the live screen still drops a name when Yahoo reports a cap under that. On the signal day, 20-day average dollar volume must exceed $50M and 20-day ADR% must exceed 5%. Delisted names are absent from a current constituent list.
  2. Trend. 9 EMA above 21 EMA above 50 EMA. The EMA you buy against has to be higher than it was 5 sessions ago. The close also has to be above the 50 EMA.
  3. Setup. The close is within 1× ADR above the rising 9 or 21 EMA, or not more than 0.25× ADR below it. When both qualify, the closer one is the trigger and anchors the stop.
  4. Entry. The signal is the close. The test buys the next session's open. If that open is more than 1.5× ADR above the trigger EMA, it is a chase and is skipped. If the open is already through the stop, there is no fill and it is not a loss.
  5. Stand down. Every month is traded.
  6. Stop. Support is the lower of the 5-session low and the trigger EMA. The stop sits at that level. If that distance is wider than 0.5× ADR%, the stop is tightened to the cap so the risk stays inside the rule.
  7. Risk. 0.40% of current equity per trade. Shares = risk dollars / stop distance. Notional is capped at 100% of equity, so a very tight stop risks less instead of using leverage. Reported R is the price result either way.
  8. Exit. Trail out when the close finishes below the 9 EMA when that was the trigger, and the 21 EMA when that was the trigger. This test fills that exit at the close. A low through the stop exits at the stop first; an open that gaps through the stop exits at the open. R = (exit − entry) / (entry − stop).
