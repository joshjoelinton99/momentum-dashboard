Core take China Mobile is a high-quality HK dividend compounder — ~6.7–6.9% yield, 75% payout with “stable-to-rising” FY26 guidance, net-cash balance sheet, and low beta. Near-term earnings are soft (VAT + legacy telecom pressure), so this is an income / defensive hold, not a growth story.

Dividend thesis

Management has lifted the payout toward ~75% and guided FY26 payout “stable-to-rising.” DPS has climbed from roughly HK$3.3–3.4 (2020–21) to HK$5.27 in FY25.

1H26 interim was RMB2.51 / HK$2.9003 (+5.5% YoY in HKD), at ~74% interim payout. Cash generation recovered sharply in 1H26 (FCF RMB53.9bn, +112% YoY), which supports the dividend even while reported profit fell 6.3%.

From 2026 interim, dividends are declared in RMB (HKD holders can elect HKD or RMB). FX can move the HKD cash amount.

Investment view

What works

  • High, growing DPS with explicit payout guidance
  • Net-cash SOE telco; beta 0.26 — portfolio ballast
  • Cheap absolute valuation (P/E ~11x, P/B ~1x)
  • Digital/AI mix shift supports medium-term earnings quality
  • Deep liquidity; easy to size in a HK dividend sleeve

What to watch

  • Legacy ARPU / communications revenue still declining
  • VAT and policy noise can hit reported EPS
  • ~75% payout leaves less cushion if profit falls further
  • RMB-declared dividends → HKD cash varies with FX
  • China regulatory / SOE governance overhang
Dividend-screener verdict Keep 0941 on a HK high-yield shortlist. At ~6.7–6.9% with rising DPS and a fortress balance sheet, it is a core income holding rather than a deep-value special situation. Size for yield stability; do not underwrite double-digit capital gains as the base case (though Street target ~HK$95 implies room if sentiment improves).
